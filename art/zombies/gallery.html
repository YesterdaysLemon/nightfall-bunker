Zombie Lineup

Six directions for Nightfall Bunker's undead, each modelled in Blender from code and staged in its own light. Spin them, hold them against their concept sheets, and mark what to keep. Your picks and notes save to this page for the next round of design.

Build your zombie

Styles are made of separable choices. Pick where each one should come from, and anything the lineup is missing.

Every model is a Python script for headless Blender 5.2 in art/zombies/. They use skin-modifier bodies posed from a skeleton, sculpting by code, and baked cloth and soft-body simulations. Concept sheets were drawn with Codex image generation, then modelled by hand-written code.

Numbers are as modelled: the height is from the model's bounds, and the render time is one 900×1200 hero frame on an RTX 4060.
